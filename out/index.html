Welcome

Welcome to Estuary Dreamz BOATEL

Experience Puducherry’s unique water-front BOATEL with serene river views, modern comforts and warm hospitality.

Estuary Dreamz BOATEL is a boat-style waterfront hotel in Puducherry, beside the backwaters and near Eden Beach, with easy access to the attractions of Puducherry.

The property offers air-conditioned rooms, curated dining and relaxing wellness options, with elegant interiors and serene estuary views.
